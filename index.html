Engineered for Perfection

Built purely for local audio lovers.

No accounts, no telemetry, no subscription locks. Pure native macOS software.

Broad Format Support

Play FLAC, ALAC, AAC, MP3, AIFF, and WAV files seamlessly. Native FLAC vorbis metadata parser for instant tag and cover art loading.

10-Band Studio EQ

Octave-spaced peaking bands (32 Hz – 16 kHz) running via MTAudioProcessingTap with limiter, clip guard, and 11 presets.

Loudness Crossfade

Dual coordinated audio engines delivering smooth 6 to 12-second crossfades. Artwork crossfades in real time with the audio.

Synced Karaoke Lyrics

Time-synced scrolling karaoke lyrics powered by local .lrc files, embedded ID3 tags, or online lookups (LRCLIB & NetEase).

Smart Artist Separation

Extracts and sorts songs by actual performers on compilations and soundtracks instead of collapsing tracks under "Various Artists".

Menu Bar & Floating

Pin to the menu bar or pop into a floating desktop window with magnetic edge-spring physics, compact mode, and multi-display support.

Studio Acoustics

Precision 10-band EQ engineered for zero distortion.

Echo includes an active peak limiter and soft clip guard that prevents harsh clipping during high-gain boosts. Includes 11 presets and separate settings for Music and Podcasts.

  • ✓ 10 octave-spaced peaking bands (32 Hz – 16 kHz, ±12 dB)
  • ✓ 11 built-in presets + custom preset creator
  • ✓ Dynamic peak limiter recovering smoothly over ~150 ms
  • ✓ Press and hold to compare unprocessed audio in real time
  • ✓ Works seamlessly over AirPlay 2 and system output
